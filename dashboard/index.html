TOKEN RADAR

Which protocol is worth farming next — and what to do about it.

How the radar works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × age × chains. The multiple is the geometric mean market-cap/TVL of the token-bearing protocols in the same category (the global geometric mean when a category has fewer than three such comps), clamped to 0.08–1.50. Each log ratio is winsorized to the global mean plus or minus 1.5 standard deviations before it is averaged, so one token whose market cap has nothing to do with its protocol’s TVL cannot set a whole category’s multiple on its own. Growth is 1 + half the 7-day TVL change, clamped to 0.75–1.50. Age is 1 + 0.15 per year tracked with no token recorded, up to 1.60. Chains add 0.02 per extra chain, up to 1.10. Nothing here is a hardcoded list of names: every number comes from the snapshot itself, and the board re-ranks from scratch on every run.

Checked, not asserted. The radar was backtested against the 128 token-bearing protocols in this snapshot that report a positive market cap: each one’s implied value was predicted with its own category’s multiple computed without it (leave-one-out), then compared with its real market cap. The predictions rank the real values with Spearman ρ = 0.41, the median prediction is off by 0.64 orders of magnitude (about 4.3×), and 23% land within twofold of the real cap. The plain median multiple this replaces scored 0.38, 0.73 and 17% on the same test. A size adjustment was tried and dropped: market-cap/TVL does fall with TVL across the whole sample, but among protocols above the $50M floor the slope is nearly flat (−0.07), so shrinking the giants’ values would be fitting noise from small protocols.

What “pre-token” means. A protocol is on this board when DefiLlama records no token for it — both its CoinGecko id and its symbol are null — and it is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. That is the record, not proof: a protocol on the list may still have a token its DefiLlama record does not carry. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: this board ranks every eligible protocol below the $50M floor too, because the early ones are the point of a radar.

What the number is. The implied value is this radar’s own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued. Farm plans give concrete steps with what they cost and how long they take; do your own checks before you put money anywhere.
