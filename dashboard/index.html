How the radar works

Implied value = TVL × size-curve multiple × growth × age × chains. The multiple comes from a curve fitted to the launched protocols in the snapshot: log10(market-cap/TVL) falls 0.41 per decade of TVL, fitted by least squares on the winsorized log ratios (log10(market-cap/TVL) = 2.48 − 0.41 × log10(TVL)). A $5M protocol prices near 0.55x its TVL, a $13.8B one near 0.02x — the market pays a falling share of TVL as protocols grow, so the giants’ values are compressed and the small ones lifted. The log ratios are winsorized to their mean plus or minus 1.5 standard deviations before the fit, so one token whose market cap has nothing to do with its protocol’s TVL cannot tilt the whole curve, and the multiple is clamped to 0.01–1.50 as a guard that never binds on this snapshot. Growth is 1 + a quarter of the 7-day TVL change, clamped to 0.88–1.25. Age is 1 + 0.10 per year tracked with no token recorded, up to 1.40. Chains add 0.02 per extra chain, up to 1.10. A category multiple was tried first and dropped: fitted category multiples rank no better than the size curve and predict worse — the category effect is mostly the size effect in disguise, since categories differ in how big their protocols are. Nothing here is a hardcoded list of names: the board re-ranks from scratch on every run.

Token odds. Each row carries the chance a protocol like it ever records a token, from the peers’ record: the share of protocols in its category that already record one, shrunk toward the snapshot-wide 54.9% with a weight of 10, so a category of one does not claim 0% or 100%. Lending sits near 76%, Risk Curators near 21%. The odds describe the category’s record; they do not predict any one protocol.

Checked, not asserted. The radar was backtested against the 128 token-bearing protocols in this snapshot that report a positive market cap, and the numbers on this page are computed by the program on every run, not typed in. Leave-one-out (each protocol predicted with a curve fitted without it): the predictions rank the real market caps with Spearman ρ = 0.41, the median prediction is off by 3.7×, and 30% land within twofold of the real cap. Held out twice (the 128 split in two by slug order, each half predicted from a curve fitted on the other): ρ = 0.45, 3.7×, 31% one way and ρ = 0.41, 3.3×, 28% the other. The plain median multiple the first version used scores 0.37, 5.2× and 20% on the same leave-one-out test, and the shrinkage-weighted category multiple it replaced scores 0.41, 4.2× and 26%.

Farm plans, costed. Each plan is about 10 transactions across 8 to 12 weeks, so the total gas cost is the chain’s per-transaction gas times 10, and the deposits are shown apart — they come back when the position closes, the gas does not. The farm score is implied value × token odds per dollar of gas spent. The verdict follows the score: 10,000,000 and up “Farm first”, 1,000,000 and up “Farm”, 100,000 and up “Farm small”, below that “Watch”. The cutoffs are the radar’s own rule, stated here; the score is what ranks the verdicts.

What “pre-token” means. A protocol is on this board when DefiLlama records no token for it — both its CoinGecko id and its symbol are null — and it is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. That is the record, not proof: a protocol on the list may still have a token its DefiLlama record does not carry. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: this board ranks every eligible protocol below the $50M floor too, because the early ones are the point of a radar. The three lists on this page account for all 758 protocols in the snapshot: 198 ranked, 144 set aside with the reason each was dropped, 416 already launched and shown as reference.

What the number is. The implied value is this radar’s own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued. Farm plans give concrete steps with what they cost and how long they take; do your own checks before you put money anywhere.
